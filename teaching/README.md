## Santa Clara University

### ISBA 3000: Quantitative Methods

**Statistics & Descriptive Analytics · Fall 2026 · 2 units**

A foundation in probability and statistics for making managerial decisions under uncertainty. Students use data to understand what happened, assess what may happen next, and improve business processes.

The course moves from descriptive statistics and probability to sampling, estimation, hypothesis testing, experiments, ANOVA, and categorical data. Simple and multiple linear regression connect these foundations to customer-value modeling, with a brief introduction to logistic regression for binary risk decisions.

**In the classroom:** advertising and customer value at the fictional Awesome Shirts, delivery-risk decisions at Awesome Delivery, and comparisons of AI assistant responses. Synthetic data, visual explanations, and optional Python notebooks support the analysis.

**Format:** five weeks, ten in-person meetings, including two guest sessions. Statistics and business application share the main classroom time. The course prepares students for ISBA 3100 and ISBA 3200.

This page is the public course overview. Enrolled students receive the current syllabus, assignments, and class materials through the course's teaching channels.

### BUS 36

**Summer 2024 · Spring 2025**

Data-driven leadership and the management of data teams. Business cases and role-based discussions connect analytics to organizational goals, hiring and team development, resource allocation, and execution.
